# Financial Consumer Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
**Tools:** Python (pandas), Power BI, Excel 

End-to-end analytics solution analyzing financial consumer data to identify high-value customer segments, evaluate product performance, and optimize regional revenue distribution.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Financial services company lacked visibility into customer profitability and product performance. Manual Excel reporting was time-consuming with no ability to segment customers or track regional trends.

**Goal:** Build automated analytics pipeline to identify revenue drivers and enable data-driven marketing and product strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Approach

**Data Preparation (Python)**
- Cleaned consumer financial dataset using pandas
- Handled missing values, removed duplicates, standardized data formats
- Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify patterns
- Created customer segmentation based on transaction behavior

**Data Modeling & Visualization (Power BI)**
- Built interactive dashboard with KPI tracking
- Created regional performance comparisons (state/geography analysis)
- Developed product-level revenue tracking
- Implemented drill-down capabilities for customer segments

**Analysis Focus**
- Customer lifetime value and segmentation
- Product revenue and satisfaction metrics
- Regional distribution and growth opportunities
- Trend analysis over time

---

## 📈 Business Impact

| Metric | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **High-Value Segment** | Identified 20% of customers driving 60% of revenue |
| **Product Performance** | Found top product outperforms others by 35% in satisfaction |
| **Regional Opportunity** | Discovered underserved suburban markets with 30% growth potential |
| **Reporting Efficiency** | Reduced manual analysis from 40 hrs/month to automated dashboards |

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

1. **Customer Segmentation:** Small segment of high-value customers contributes majority of revenue (Pareto principle confirmed)
2. **Product Excellence:** Investment Portfolio A consistently outperforms in returns and customer satisfaction
3. **Revenue Concentration:** 70% of revenue from urban areas - suburban expansion opportunity exists
4. **Seasonality:** Revenue spikes during tax season and year-end financial planning periods
5. **Customer Satisfaction:** Average rating 4.8/5 reflects strong service quality

**Recommendations:** Focus retention efforts on high-value segment, optimize Investment Portfolio A, expand marketing to suburban markets, increase campaigns during peak seasons.
